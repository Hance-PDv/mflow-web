Lumina Sound

Lumina Sound is a refined music player giving you total control over your audio library through a smooth, ad-free interface tailored to your preferences.

Features

YouTube Music Integration

Log in directly with your YouTube Music account to stream your music, access your library, and enjoy seamless playback.

High-Fidelity Audio

Delivers crisp, rich sound playback with dynamic audio output for an immersive listening experience.

Real-Time Synced Lyrics

Displays perfectly synchronized lyrics with smooth line-by-line scrolling that tracks your song precisely.

Smart Sleep Timer

Set custom countdowns with quick increment controls (±5 min) to auto-stop playback seamlessly.

Instant Favorites

Save and access your most-cherished tracks instantly with a single tap.

Custom Playlists

Effortlessly create, edit, and organize personalized song collections tailored to your vibe.

Listening Insights

Track your music habits with detailed stats, including total listen time, top-played tracks, and daily trends.
